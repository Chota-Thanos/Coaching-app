# Mains-style subjective questions — `mains_pyq`

Write a question with a full model answer, in the genuine UPSC Mains style.

## Requirements, per question

- A **directive verb** used precisely and consistently with the answer you
  write: Discuss, Examine, Critically examine, Elucidate, Comment, Evaluate.
  The directive determines the structure — "critically examine" demands both
  a case for and against; "discuss" is more open; don't write a one-sided
  answer to a directive that calls for balance.
- **Marks and word limit that match convention**: 10 marks / 150 words, or
  15 marks / 250 words are the two standard pairings — pick whichever fits
  the question's scope, and keep the model answer close to the stated limit.
- **Answer approach** — the skeleton: what the introduction should establish,
  what each body paragraph should cover, what the conclusion should do. Write
  this as a short structural outline, separate from the model answer itself.
- **Model answer** — a complete answer written to the stated word limit,
  actually following the skeleton above, with real data, committee names and
  examples woven in where they support the argument (not tacked on as a list).

## Balance

Where the directive invites critique ("critically examine", "evaluate"),
the model answer must genuinely present both the case for and the case
against before reaching a conclusion. A one-sided "critical" answer is a
common and easy mistake — check your own draft against this before finishing.
